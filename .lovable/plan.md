# Nikhil Sharma — Premium Personal Brand Redesign

## Direction
Build the selected **Diagonal Evidence** concept as a complete, responsive one-page website: cinematic portraiture, carbon and warm ivory surfaces, restrained saffron accents, Instrument Serif headlines, Work Sans body copy, and editorial rather than decorative luxury.

The result will take the core strengths of the Manu reference—strong hierarchy, personal storytelling, immersive imagery, and evidence of experience—without copying its composition, star field, route graphics, wording, or section sequence.

## Page structure
1. **Minimal navigation** — Nikhil Sharma identity, Story, Ventures, Method, Recognition, and one enquiry action.
2. **Portrait-led opening** — monumental Nikhil portrait, “The architect of compounding growth,” concise positioning, and verified metrics.
3. **Founder story** — an editorial biography focused on business growth, digital transformation, mentorship, and his former Truecaller brand ambassador recognition.
4. **Venture ecosystem** — a horizontal visual presentation of real ventures from the official site, such as AnandBodh, IconsBase, EaseHire, Fabulous.Media, GoCommercially, and Vanishing India; no fictional companies.
5. **InvestorReady™ method** — a clean four-stage transformation narrative using only claims supported by official content.
6. **Recognition and press** — verified 2019 awards, Truecaller recognition, and the two named cover stories from the official site.
7. **Focused closing invitation** — direct links to Nikhil’s official website and contact destination, followed by a minimal footer.

## Imagery
- Use Nikhil’s real official portrait for the opening and story sections; do not generate or alter his likeness.
- Reuse suitable existing imagery and venture logos from the supplied GitHub project and official sources where licensing and URLs permit.
- Use atmospheric business imagery only as supporting material, never as a substitute for Nikhil’s identity.
- Maintain dark cinematic grading, warm directional light, visible detail, and consistent crops across desktop and mobile.

If bespoke supporting images are still needed after the first implementation, provide exact generation prompts for those scenes only.

## Interaction and finish
- Smooth in-page navigation and clear keyboard focus states.
- Restrained opening reveals, a subtle light sweep across the metrics panel, and gentle venture movement.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Ensure the horizontal venture presentation remains touch-scrollable and readable on mobile.
- Preserve stable layout dimensions so imagery, metrics, and labels do not shift or overlap.

## Accuracy and safeguards
- Use facts verified from `nikhilsharma.com` and `elevatedindia.com`.
- Resolve conflicting figures conservatively and avoid unsupported claims from the existing GitHub copy.
- Do not invent awards, press names, clients, contact details, or company outcomes.
- Link outward rather than building non-functional forms or fabricated booking flows.

## Technical implementation
- Replace the blank home screen with the finished page in the existing TanStack app.
- Add the selected palette and typography as semantic design tokens in the global stylesheet.
- Load fonts through the page head, and add unique title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata to the home page.
- Build focused React sections and keep all controls accessible.
- Verify the result at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, links, image loading, motion preferences, text wrapping, and visual overlap.
